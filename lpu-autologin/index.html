Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

AES-GCM 256-bit Vault
Passwords are encrypted locally with a per-install key. They never leave your device and are never stored in plain-text.
3 LPU Portals
Internet Gateway (internet.lpu.in + 10.10.0.1), MyClass/UMS (redirects to CodeTantra), and OAS — all covered with one set of credentials.
Real-time Network Monitor
Live gateway ping with status codes. Shows your public IP when connected. Colour-coded orb for instant status.
Instant & Offline-ready
Zero CDN dependencies. Font bundled locally. Works on restricted campus networks and offline without degradation.
Aura Theme System
Four accent colour themes — Orange, Blue, Green, Purple. Preference persisted across sessions.
Master ON/OFF Toggle
Instantly disable auto-login without removing credentials. Re-enable with one tap. Persisted via local storage.
